Questions about Digitaldoc

What the free account includes and how documents, sharing, and paid features work.

What can I do for free in Digitaldoc?

The free account includes document organization in folders, sharing with links, and 1 GB of storage. Upload, view, download, and merge PDFs without a subscription or AI credits. Choose the file order when merging; Digitaldoc saves a new PDF and keeps the originals.

Which files use my storage?

Documents, uploaded and merged PDFs, images, and DICOM and STL files share the same storage quota. Extend the included 1 GB with a storage subscription. If you purchase multiple tiers, the largest tier applies; their capacities are not added together.

Which features require a subscription?

The visual template editor, previews of PDFs generated from templates, certain field types, and additional modules are purchased separately. The HTML template editor and downloading documents as PDFs are free. The pricing calculator shows the monthly cost of selected features; review the options in the app before paying.

Do recipients need an account to open a shared link?

Recipients can open a shared file or folder in their browser without a Digitaldoc account. Create the link in the app and send it to the people who need access. Check the included documents and sharing options before distributing the link.

How is AI usage billed?

Chat AI access does not require a monthly subscription, but generating responses uses tokens. DigitaldocAI deducts usage from prepaid credits. With your own OpenAI key, the provider bills your account. Document scanning and Contexto have separate subscriptions, with AI usage charged in addition.
